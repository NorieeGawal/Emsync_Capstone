# EMSYNC User Manual

This guide explains the main tasks for HR, managers, and employees.

## Sign in and get around

1. Open the EMSYNC web address provided by your company.
2. Sign in with the email and password issued to you.
3. Use the menu on the left to open the pages available to your role.
4. Use the profile menu near the top-right to update your profile or password.
5. Select **Log out** when you finish, especially on a shared computer.

After three failed sign-in attempts, you must wait one minute before trying again. If you cannot sign in or a page says you do not have permission, contact HR or your system administrator.

## HR

HR manages departments, accounts, attendance, KPI records, and reports.

### Set up departments and accounts

1. Open **Departments** and add the departments your organization uses.
2. Open **Employees**, then select **Add Account**.
3. Enter the person's name, email, password, role, and department.
4. Check the role and department carefully, then save.
5. Use **Edit** to update an account. **Delete** moves it to **Archived Accounts**, where it can be restored for one month.

### Record attendance

1. Open **Attendance**. The attendance page opens the bulk attendance screen.
2. Check the company **Time In** and **Time Out** schedule. Update and save it if necessary.
3. Choose the date. You can filter by department or search for an employee.
4. For each employee, select **Present**, **Late**, **Absent**, or **Excused**. Enter time-in and time-out in 24-hour format (for example, `08:30`).
5. Check the entries and select **Save Attendance**.

When a time-in is entered for a Present or Late record, the system compares it with the saved schedule and can set the status to Present or Late automatically. Saving attendance again for the same employee and date updates that day's record.

### Enter KPI scores

1. Open **KPI Management** and find the employee or manager.
2. Select **Encode** to enter scores for one person, or **Bulk Entry** to apply one KPI score to several people.
3. Select the correct month.
4. Enter scores from 0 to 100 for **Work Quality**, **Efficiency**, and **Timeliness**.
5. For **Productivity**, enter the target workload and completed workload. The system calculates the score as completed ÷ target × 100, capped at 100%. For example, 18 completed out of 20 is 90%.
6. Add optional remarks, check the selected person or people and month, then save.

Bulk entry applies a selected score to each checked person. For Productivity, enter a separate target and completed count for every selected person.

**Attendance** and **Punctuality** are calculated from attendance records, not typed in as KPI scores:

- Attendance = Present or Late days ÷ all logged days.
- Punctuality = Present (on-time) days ÷ Present or Late days.

The overall score uses these weights:

| KPI | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Productivity | 15% |
| Efficiency | 10% |
| Work Quality | 25% |
| Timeliness | 20% |
| Attendance | 20% |
| Punctuality | 10% |

Missing scores count as **0** in the overall score. Missing attendance logs also produce 0 for Attendance and Punctuality. Check that the selected month has complete records before relying on a total. Saving scores for the same person, KPI, and month updates the existing entry.

### Delete an incorrect saved KPI entry (HR)

1. In **KPI Management**, open **Delete KPI Scores** beside **Bulk Entry**, then find the employee or manager and choose the review period.
2. Review the employee, period, and scores. Enter a deletion reason, then confirm to delete the entire entry or only the selected KPI criterion.
3. Use **Encode** to enter and save the corrected scores. The deletion is saved in KPI history with the actor, reason, values, and timestamp.

### Correct KPI scores (HR)

1. Use **Edit KPI** beside an employee or manager to correct existing scores; enter a reason when saving changes.
2. To remove an incorrect score, open **Delete KPI Scores** beside **Bulk Entry** and select **Delete KPI** for the relevant employee.
3. Provide a reason when deleting. The action and previous values are recorded in KPI history.
4. The **Delete KPI Scores** bulk action removes scores for the selected people and review period only.

### Review employees and download reports

- On the **Dashboard**, filter by month or department and search for employees.
- Open an employee profile to review their scores, attendance, and feedback. HR can add feedback there.
- Select **Download PDF** on an employee profile for that employee's report.
- Select **Download Summary PDF** on the dashboard for the selected month and department.

## Manager

Managers can view their department's team performance.

1. Open **Dashboard** to review department performance for the displayed month.
2. Open **My Team** to find an employee in your department.
3. Open an employee profile to review KPI scores, recent attendance, and existing feedback.
4. To leave feedback, enter it in **Performance Feedback** and select **Add Feedback**.

Managers can only view and leave feedback for employees in their assigned department. Contact HR if an employee is missing or assigned to the wrong department.

## Employee

Employees can review their own performance information.

- **Dashboard:** View KPI scores, the overall rating and recent score trend, performance feedback, and recent attendance.
- **Profile:** Update personal information, change your password, or update your profile photo.

Employees cannot enter or change KPI scores or attendance records. Contact HR if information looks incorrect or is missing.

## Quick reminders

- Confirm the month, employee, and department before saving records.
- A low overall score may mean that a KPI score or attendance record is missing; missing values count as zero.
- Keep your password private and do not share your account.
- If an account, department, attendance entry, or score needs correction, contact HR.
